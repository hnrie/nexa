NEXA - PRIVACY POLICY
Site: chat.evilion.xyz
Run by: Nora (in the app: @nora) - contact: evilionx3@proton.me
Effective: 2026-09-20

This policy lists exactly what this site holds. It is written against
the actual database, so if something is not listed here, there is no
place for it and nothing to hand over.

1. THE SHORT VERSION

- Your messages, files, and calls are encrypted end to end. I cannot
  read them and I cannot monitor anything you do inside a chat.
- The ONLY content I can actually see is profile information you
  choose to make public (your username, display name, avatar, banner,
  about me, pronouns, status, and similar fields), plus the delivery
  details the server needs (who sent what, where, and when) and
  public images (avatars, banners, server icons, custom emoji,
  stickers).
- No email, no phone number, no real name, no payment details, no
  location, no contacts, no analytics, and no push tokens are ever
  collected.

2. WHAT IS ENCRYPTED (I CANNOT READ IT)

- Message text and edits. Stored only as ciphertext.
- File contents, file names, file types, dimensions, and per file
  keys (the key travels inside the encrypted message).
- Call audio and video. Calls are not recorded and are never written
  to disk.
- Your settings (themes, notification choices, mutes). Only the size,
  the version number, and the last written time can be seen.
- Your password and recovery key. Your password never leaves your
  device. Your browser only sends a derived check value. Your
  recovery key is shown once in your browser and never stored
  anywhere.
- Your private identity key and your conversation keys. Your private
  key sits on the server only wrapped by your password or recovery
  key. Conversation keys sit on the server only sealed separately to
  each member. I hold no usable key of any kind. There is no spare
  key, no master key, and no escrow.

There is no setting, admin action, or software change I could make
that would decrypt content already sent, and no way to deliver future
content in readable form.

3. WHAT I CAN SEE

a) Profile information (public by design, typed by you):
   username, display name, avatar and banner images, about me,
   pronouns, accent color, custom status and expiry, presence
   preference, DM privacy setting, per server nicknames and avatars,
   account id, creation and last change times, admin and disabled
   flags. None of this is identity checked.

b) Public images (stored as uploaded, can be handed over if ordered):
   avatars, banners, server icons, custom emoji, stickers.

c) Delivery details (needed to send and notify, not content):
   message id, channel, author, type, timestamps, edit time, reply
   target, mentions (user, role, and everyone flags are kept outside
   the ciphertext so the server knows who to notify), file presence
   and sizes, reaction values and counts, pins, read positions, and
   poll vote positions (not the question or option text, which is
   encrypted).

d) Membership and structure (typed by users, stored as typed):
   friends, requests, and blocks, server memberships and roles, DM
   and group participants, channel names, topics, and structure,
   invite codes you made (uses and expiry, but not who used them),
   bans, and per community audit log entries.

e) Sessions: one entry per signed in device. That is the session id,
   the IP address noted ONCE at sign in (never updated), the browser
   or app description, and the sign in and last active times. Entries
   are deleted on sign out or expiry. This is the only place an IP is
   written. A locked subchat also notes which sessions typed its
   password (the password itself is not kept, it only unlocks the
   screen, messages stay encrypted under the conversation key).
   Guest call links belong to the link, not to your account.

f) Login checks (values not handed over, listed to close the point):
   the password check value, the password stretching salt and
   settings, your public key, your wrapped private key (cannot be
   opened without your password), and the wrapped recovery copy with
   its check value. None of these is a password and none can be
   turned into one.

g) Day to day running: unfinished upload progress (expected and
   received sizes), crash reports (error kind, code location, app
   version, message, never any chat or file content), one site wide
   monthly data total (not per account), and server request logs
   (page, result, size, duration, no IP recorded there).

4. WHAT IS NEVER COLLECTED

No email address. No phone number. No legal name, birth date, age,
or ID documents. No payment details. No registration IP or last
known IP field beyond the per session sign in IP above. No IP or
access history. No online time history. No call records (who called
whom, for how long). No device or advertising IDs and no push
tokens. No location. No address book imports. No search words, link
clicks, analytics, or tracking of any kind. No drafts, backups, or
unsent copies. Search runs on your device using messages it already
decrypted.

5. WHO SEES WHAT

- Other people see your public profile, your messages once their app
  decrypts them, and the communities and channels you share.
- I (one person) see sections 3a to 3g above and nothing else.
- No third parties, no advertisers, no analytics companies. Nothing
  is sold or shared, except as forced by a valid legal order (see
  Terms Section 5), and even then only what exists above. Content
  still cannot be handed over.

6. DELETION

- Deleted messages, files, and accounts are removed from the
  database. No trash bin or archive is kept.
- Deleting an account overwrites its profile, and deletes its
  sessions, relationships, settings, key grants, and second factor
  rows. Past messages stay behind, still encrypted, marked as
  written by a deleted user.
- Backups, if kept for disaster recovery, hold the same encrypted
  content under the same limits.
